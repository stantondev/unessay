## CORRECTIONS NEEDED (apply to data files)

### Critical
1. **Kituwah mound "170 ft"** — This is the **diameter**, not the height. The mound is only ~5-6 feet tall, but ~170 feet across. The brief likely confused height and diameter.

### Important
2. **Cherokee Constitution date** — Brief says July 26, 1827. Most sources say **July 24, 1827**.
3. **Deer clan transliteration** — "Aniawi" should be **"Anikawi"** and syllabary should be **ᎠᏂᎧᏫ** (not ᎠᏂᎠᏫ).
4. **Fort Marr location** — Brief says "SE Bradley County, TN." Actually in **Polk County, TN** (near Benton).
5. **Red Clay coordinates** — Brief gives (35.08, -84.86). Should be approximately **(35.10, -84.96)** — the brief's coords are ~6-7 miles off.
6. **New Echota coordinates** — Brief gives (34.53, -84.70). Closer to **(34.60, -84.79)**.
7. **Kituwah coordinates** — Not in brief (marked "—"). Should be approximately **(35.43, -83.39)** near Bryson City, NC.

### Minor / Clarifications
8. **Indian Removal Act date** — May 28, 1830 is Jackson's signing date. The House vote was **May 26, 1830**. Clarify in app text.
9. **Treaty of New Echota "ratified by single vote"** — More precisely: Senate voted 31-15, exactly the two-thirds minimum (one vote above threshold), not a one-vote overall margin.
10. **Three-Fifths Clause argument** — The vote breakdown (free states 41-82 against; slave states 61-15 for) adds up correctly to 102-97. The counterfactual argument is legitimate scholarship but should be attributed rather than stated as simple fact.
11. **"No centralized government before 1794"** — The 1794 date is non-standard. Scholars more commonly cite the period 1808-1827 for formal centralization. Needs a source or reframe.
12. **Mantle Rock "11 detachments waited"** — Likely overstated. Multiple detachments passed through, but the two-week camp is best documented for the Hildebrand detachment.
13. **George Hicks departure "Sept 7, 1838"** — Disputed; some sources give October dates. Flag as approximate.
14. **"John Golden Ross" as detachment co-conductor** — Not a standard designation in most sources. Typically listed as part of the Ross family party.
15. **Cahokia "20,000-40,000"** — The high end (40,000) includes the greater metro area. The city core is more commonly estimated at 10,000-20,000.
16. **Americas pre-contact "~100 million"** — This is a higher-end scholarly estimate (Denevan). Should note the range of estimates.
17. **Cherokee population of 6,000 in Missouri by 1817** — May be overstated; needs a specific source citation.
18. **"2021: Cherokee Nation Supreme Court removes 'by blood' from constitution"** — More precisely about affirming Freedmen citizenship rights (Nash/Vann ruling), not simply removing language.
19. **EBCI land "~56,000 acres"** — May be slightly low; some sources cite ~57,000 acres depending on recent acquisitions.
20. **Treaty of Fort Jackson "~4 million acres"** — This is the Cherokee-claimed portion of the ~23 million total acres ceded. Flag as approximate.

## VERIFIED VIA WEB SEARCH (April 2026)

1. **Nikwasi Mound transfer** — CONFIRMED. Deed-signing ceremony held **February 26, 2026** (matches brief exactly). Note: Cherokee sources use the name **"Noquisiyi"** (not Nikwasi). First mound returned to an Indigenous tribe in North Carolina. Sources: [Cherokee One Feather](https://theonefeather.com/2026/02/27/it-is-a-part-of-us-noquisiyi-mound-deed-signing-ceremony-held/), [BPR](https://www.bpr.org/politics-government/2026-02-27/noquisiyi-nikwasi-mound-officially-returns-to-the-eastern-band-of-cherokee-indians)

2. **Cherokee Nation enrollment** — CONFIRMED at **466,118** (late 2024 demographics data). Distribution matches brief: ~283,000 in OK, ~29,400 in TX, ~28,000 in CA. Source: [Cherokee Nation demographics / The World Data](https://theworlddata.com/cherokee-native-american-tribe/)

3. **Kituwah mound dimensions** — CONFIRMED the error in the brief. Mound is **5 feet high and 170 feet in diameter** (originally 15-20 feet tall, reduced by farming). The brief says "170 ft mound" which misleadingly suggests height. Must correct to "170 ft diameter mound." Source: [NC DNCR](https://www.dncr.nc.gov/blog/2024/01/23/kituwah-q-57)
